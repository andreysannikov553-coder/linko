How it works

For guests and for your staff

  1. Every table gets its own QR code with the table number.
  2. Guests open the menu in the browser, no app needed: photos, descriptions, prices and the order total in their language.
  3. The order instantly arrives in your staff Telegram chat: dishes, table number and total.
  4. Prices and sold-out items are changed in one place and update on every table at once.
